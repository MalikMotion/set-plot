Free during the open beta. Or try it now without an account.

Brand film · Interview

See the light before you rig it

Place lights, flags, bounce and diffusion, and watch the beams and shadows follow. Try it here: move the light, the frame and the flag.

Draw the room in a minute

Start from a room preset and drag a corner to fit what you measured on the recce. Windows, doors and curtains move with their walls. Give it a go.

Notes, to-dos and ideas in one place

Every set-up has its own board. Write notes, tick off to-dos and branch out ideas, right next to the plot they belong to. Add a few here and it zooms out to keep them all in view.

Made for small crews.

For the people who plan the shot and then go and light it.

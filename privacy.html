Privacy

What Set Plot stores, where it's kept, and how to get it deleted. In plain words.

Last updated 10 November 2026

The short version. You need a free account to use Set Plot. We store your email, name and a few answers about your work, plus the plots, storyboards, photos and audio you create, so they follow you to other devices and can be shared with your crew. The data is kept in the EU. There are no ads, no third-party tracking and no selling of data, and you can have everything deleted at any time.

Who is responsible

Set Plot is made by Malik Motion, Randers, Denmark. Malik Motion is the data controller for the information described here. Questions or requests: tobias@malikmotion.dk.

What we store, and why

Alpha listThe email you type into the sign-up form, the date, and the invite code if you came through a friend's link. Used to send you news about the alpha and when accounts or plans change. Based on your consent.
Your accountYour name, email address and a login record, plus your answers to the questions when you join: what describes you best, what you mostly shoot, your usual crew size and, if you choose to say, how you heard about Set Plot. We look at these answers only as totals, to understand who uses Set Plot. You can change them any time under Account → Edit profile. If you sign in with Google, Microsoft or Apple, also the name and profile picture they share. Used to sign you in. Needed to provide the service you asked for.
Your workWhen you're signed in: your setups, plots, notes, drawings and storyboards, plus the photos and audio (including voice recordings) you add to them. Stored only so you can open them on your devices. Needed to provide the service.
Shared projectsWhen you share a shoot, the people you add can see everything in it, plus your name and email address in the list of members. You can see theirs. If you add someone who has no account yet, we keep their email address as a pending invitation until they sign up or you cancel it. While you have a shared setup open, the others in the project see your name, where your cursor is and what you have selected, live. Cursor positions are passed straight through and never stored. Chat messages you write in a shared setup are stored with that project and visible to its members. When you @mention someone, they get a notification showing your name and the start of the message.
Versions and view linksFor setups saved to your account we keep earlier versions of the setup (automatic snapshots at most every 20 minutes, the 60 newest are kept, plus any named versions) so you and your project can go back. Versions are deleted with the setup. If you create a view-only link, anyone who has the link can see that setup and its images until you turn the link off.
Media poolPhotos and audio you import into a shoot's media pool are stored with your account, or in the project if the shoot is shared, so everyone on the project can use them. Removing a clip from the pool doesn't remove it from storyboards that already use it.
NewsletterIf you tick “Get offers and new features first” when you sign up or in Edit profile, we store that choice and when you made it, and may email you about Set Plot. Untick it under Edit profile, or use the unsubscribe link in any email, and we stop.
Invite emailsWhen you add someone to a shoot, we send one email to the address you typed, with your name and the shoot’s name. We log that it was sent so the same person isn’t emailed over and over.
Bug reports and ideasWhen you send a bug report or feature idea, we keep your message, your account (or the email you type, if you add one) and, if you leave the box ticked, your browser, screen size and app version, so we can reproduce the problem. Only the Set Plot team can read them.
Usage eventsWhen you're signed in, the app notes which features are used, for example "animatic played" or "storyboard saved", with the time and your account, and records technical error messages if something breaks. Never the content of your work. Used to see which features matter and to fix problems. Based on our legitimate interest in running and improving the service.
In your browserThe app saves your work, settings and sign-in session in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device and isn't used for tracking. When you sign out, your account's work is put away and only comes back when that same account signs in again, so someone else signing in on the same browser can't see it. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Your plots, photos and audio are private to your account. The database only lets the signed-in owner read or change them. Nobody else can see them, and we don't look at them unless you ask us to help with a problem.

Where it's kept

  • Supabase hosts the database, storage and sign-in. Our project runs in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • GitHub Pages serves the website and app. Like any web host, it handles basic request data such as your IP address to deliver the pages.
  • Google provides the “Sign in with Google” button. Your browser loads it from Google when the sign-in window is open, and if you use it, Google shares your name, email and profile picture with us.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the typeface and the sign-in library, so your browser contacts them when the page loads.

Some of these providers are US companies. Where data may leave the EU, they rely on the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

Alpha-list emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you. Account data and your work are kept while you have an account. You can delete your account yourself any time under Account → Delete account: your account, setups, photos and audio are deleted straight away. Shared projects you started are handed to another member so your crew keeps them. We keep an anonymous note that an account was deleted (when, how long it existed, the answers about your work and the reason you chose, if any), with no name or email. Things you delete in the app are removed from the cloud when the app next syncs.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, get a copy, correct it, have it deleted, or withdraw your consent to alpha emails at any time. Just email tobias@malikmotion.dk from the address you signed up with. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and, if you're on the alpha list or have an account, tell you by email.

Privacy

What Set Plot stores, where it's kept, and how to get it deleted. In plain words.

Last updated 10 November 2026

The short version. Without an account, everything you make stays in your own browser. If you join the beta or sign in, we store your email and, when signed in, the plots, storyboards, photos and audio you create, so they can follow you to other devices. The data is kept in the EU. There are no ads, no third-party tracking and no selling of data, and you can have everything deleted at any time.

Who is responsible

Set Plot is made by Malik Motion, Randers, Denmark. Malik Motion is the data controller for the information described here. Questions or requests: tobias@malikmotion.dk.

What we store, and why

Beta listThe email you type into the sign-up form, the date, and the invite code if you came through a friend's link. Used to send you news about the beta and when accounts or plans change. Based on your consent.
Your accountYour name, email address and a login record, plus your answers to the questions when you join: what describes you best, what you mostly shoot, your usual crew size and, if you choose to say, how you heard about Set Plot. We look at these answers only as totals, to understand who uses Set Plot. You can change them any time under Account → Edit profile. If you sign in with Google, Microsoft or Apple, also the name and profile picture they share. Used to sign you in. Needed to provide the service you asked for.
Your workWhen you're signed in: your setups, plots, notes, drawings and storyboards, plus the photos and audio (including voice recordings) you add to them. Stored only so you can open them on your devices. Needed to provide the service.
Shared projectsWhen you share a shoot, the people you add can see everything in it, plus your name and email address in the list of members. You can see theirs. If you add someone who has no account yet, we keep their email address as a pending invitation until they sign up or you cancel it. While you have a shared setup open, the others in the project see your name, where your cursor is and what you have selected, live. Cursor positions are passed straight through and never stored.
Usage eventsWhen you're signed in, the app notes which features are used, for example "animatic played" or "storyboard saved", with the time and your account, and records technical error messages if something breaks. Never the content of your work. Used to see which features matter and to fix problems. Based on our legitimate interest in running and improving the service.
In your browserThe app saves your work, settings and sign-in session in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device and isn't used for tracking. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Your plots, photos and audio are private to your account. The database only lets the signed-in owner read or change them. Nobody else can see them, and we don't look at them unless you ask us to help with a problem.

Where it's kept

  • Supabase hosts the database, storage and sign-in. Our project runs in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • GitHub Pages serves the website and app. Like any web host, it handles basic request data such as your IP address to deliver the pages.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the typeface and the sign-in library, so your browser contacts them when the page loads.

Some of these providers are US companies. Where data may leave the EU, they rely on the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

Beta-list emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you. Account data and your work are kept while you have an account, and deleted within 30 days of you deleting it or asking us to. Things you delete in the app are removed from the cloud when the app next syncs.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, get a copy, correct it, have it deleted, or withdraw your consent to beta emails at any time. Just email tobias@malikmotion.dk from the address you signed up with. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and, if you're on the beta list or have an account, tell you by email.

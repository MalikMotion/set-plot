High-key vs low-key lighting: what’s the difference?

By Malik Motion6 min readUpdated 11 November 2026

Quick answer

High-key lighting is bright, even and low in contrast, with a light background and soft shadows: think commercials and sitcoms. Low-key lighting is dark and high in contrast, with deep shadows and little fill: think noir and thrillers. The difference comes down to the key-to-fill ratio and how bright the background is.

"Key" here doesn’t mean the key light. It describes the overall tone of the image: high-key images sit mostly in the bright end of the histogram, low-key images in the dark end.

High-key: how to set it up

Light the background separately and make it 1–1.5 stops brighter than the face so it goes clean white. Use a large soft key close to the camera and plenty of fill, around 2:1. Keep shadows soft and short.

Low-key: how to set it up

Use one hard or medium source, often from the side or behind. Keep the fill low (8:1 or none), keep the background dark and use flags and negative fill to stop light bouncing around the room. What you leave dark matters as much as what you light.

Choosing between them

  • High-key: product, beauty, comedy, corporate, anything that should feel safe and open.
  • Low-key: drama, horror, thrillers, music videos, anything that should feel tense or intimate.

Step by step

  1. Decide the toneChoose high-key or low-key from the story or the brand, before you choose lights.
  2. Set the backgroundLight it bright for high-key, keep it dark for low-key.
  3. Set the ratioFill at about 2:1 for high-key, 8:1 or none for low-key.
  4. Control the spillAdd bounce for high-key, flags and negative fill for low-key.

Gear and common mistakes

What you need

  • High-key: large soft key, fill, 2 background lights, white backdrop
  • Low-key: 1 key, flags, negative fill, dark backdrop

Common mistakes

  • High-key: the background not bright enough, so it goes grey.
  • Low-key: no separation, so the subject disappears into the dark.

Frequently asked questions

What is high-key lighting?

A bright, low-contrast lighting style with soft shadows and a light background.

What is low-key lighting?

A dark, high-contrast lighting style with deep shadows and little fill.

Is low-key lighting the same as underexposure?

No. In low-key lighting the important parts are correctly exposed; it’s the shadows and the background that are kept dark on purpose.

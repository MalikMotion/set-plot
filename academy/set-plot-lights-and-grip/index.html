Placing lights, grip and beams in Set Plot

By Malik Motion6 min readUpdated 11 November 2026

Quick answer

Add a light from Lights (1), drag it into place and drag its handle to aim it. Select it to set the fixture, modifier (M), power and Kelvin, and use the slider above the light for quick power changes. Press B to show or hide beams, I for fixture specs, and [ / ] to turn in 15° steps.

Lights in Set Plot behave like the real thing: a softbox throws a wide soft beam, a Fresnel a narrow one, and the beam colour follows the colour temperature. That makes the plan useful on its own, before anyone has set up a stand.

Aiming and rotating

Drag a light to move it and drag the round handle to aim it. Hold Shift while aiming to snap to 15° steps, or press [ and ]. With several things selected, press R to aim or rotate the whole group around its centre.

Modifiers, power and colour

Press M with a light selected to choose a modifier: softbox, grid, dome, barn doors and more. The beam shape updates straight away. Set colour temperature in Kelvin or add a gel in the inspector; tinted beams make mixed colour easy to read on the plan.

Grip

Frames, bounces, negative fill, flags and V-flats live under Grip (2). Standard sizes such as 4×4, 6×6 and 8×8 are built in, and the material (grid cloth, Ultrabounce, solid black) goes on the plot sheet and the gear list.

Beams and specs

Press B to hide the beams when the plan gets busy, and I to show or hide the fixture specs under each label. Press Shift + H to highlight the selection and dim everything else.

Power

Add an outlet from Set (5), then drag from a light to the outlet to plug it in. Set Plot draws the cable and adds the load to the power budget, which you can export as a sheet for the gaffer.

Keyboard shortcuts

These are the defaults on a Mac (⌘, ⌥) and on Windows (Ctrl, Alt). Press ? in the app to see your own, including any you’ve customized.

Step by step

  1. Add a lightOpen Lights with 1 and drag a fixture onto the plan.
  2. Aim itDrag the aim handle toward the talent.
  3. Shape itPress M to add a modifier and set power and Kelvin.
  4. Add gripAdd a frame, bounce or flag from Grip with 2.
  5. Plug inAdd an outlet and drag each light to it for the power budget.

Tips and tricks

  • Label lights by their job (Key, Fill, Back): the label is what the crew reads.
  • Duplicate a light with ⌘/Ctrl + D to keep its settings.
  • Lock finished parts of the plan with ⌘/Ctrl + L so they don’t move by accident.
  • Group a light with its frame (⌘/Ctrl + G) and move them as one.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change a light’s beam angle?

Yes. Pick a different modifier, or set the beam angle and throw in the inspector.

Does Set Plot calculate power?

Yes. Plug lights into outlets and export the power budget sheet.

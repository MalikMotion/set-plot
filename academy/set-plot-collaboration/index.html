Working with your crew in Set Plot: sharing, chat and comments

By Malik Motion6 min readUpdated 11 November 2026

Quick answer

Click Share to add people to a shoot by email with view, edit or admin rights; they get an email invite, even if they don’t have an account yet. Plan together live with cursors and follow mode, talk in the chat with @mentions and #tags for objects, leave comment threads, save named versions, and send clients a view-only link.

Lighting plans get better when the gaffer, the director and the producer can all see them before the day. Set Plot is built for that: one shoot, everyone on the same page, in real time.

Sharing a shoot

Open Share and type an email address. Choose Can view, Can edit or Admin, and whether they can invite others. Everyone in a shoot sees every setup in it, with its photos, storyboards and audio. People without an account get an email with a sign-up link, and the shoot is waiting when they join.

Live together

You see each other’s cursors and selections as you work. You can follow a collaborator’s view, which is handy on a call.

Chat, mentions and tags

The chat sits at the bottom right and grows with the conversation. Type @ to mention someone; they get a notification. Type # to tag an object or card; the tag stays in sync if the object is renamed, and clicking it highlights and selects the object. Search the chat to find an old decision.

Comments and reactions

Add a comment card (Shift + 3) next to the thing it’s about. Comments have replies, emoji reactions and a resolve tick, so open notes don’t get lost.

Versions and view-only links

Admins can save a named version before a big change. Anyone can open a version read-only in a split view and copy objects back into the live plan. For clients, create a view-only link: they can look without an account, and you can turn the link off at any time.

Keyboard shortcuts

These are the defaults on a Mac (⌘, ⌥) and on Windows (Ctrl, Alt). Press ? in the app to see your own, including any you’ve customized.

Step by step

  1. Share the shootClick Share and add your crew by email.
  2. Set the rightsGive each person view, edit or admin rights.
  3. Plan liveWork on the plan together and follow each other’s view.
  4. Leave notesUse comments and the chat with @mentions and #tags.
  5. Send to the clientCreate a view-only link for people outside the crew.

Tips and tricks

  • Resolve comment threads when they’re done so the board stays readable.
  • Save a named version before changing a setup the director already approved.
  • Notifications for mentions and invites show in the bell at the top right.

Frequently asked questions

Can clients see a plan without an account?

Yes. Create a view-only link from Share. They can look but not change anything.

Do invited people need an account?

They get an email either way. People without an account are sent to sign up, and the shoot is waiting for them.

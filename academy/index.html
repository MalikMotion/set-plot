About the Academy

What is the Set Plot Academy?

Free lessons on film and photo lighting, camera coverage and shoot planning. Every lesson includes a real lighting diagram you can move around and open in Set Plot.

Who is it for?

Beginner and intermediate filmmakers, videographers, photographers and gaffers who want to understand why a setup works, not just copy it.

Is it free?

Yes. The lessons are free to read, and Set Plot is free during the open beta.

Can I use the diagrams in my own work?

Yes. Open any diagram in Set Plot to save a copy, change it to fit your location and export it for your crew.

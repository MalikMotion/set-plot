11 November 2026

New
Invite-only alpha

New accounts now need an invite during the alpha: an invite link from someone who uses Set Plot, being added to a shared shoot, or a spot from the waitlist. Everyone else can join the waitlist from the website or the sign-up screen, and gets an email when they're in. Existing accounts aren't affected.

New
Present live to your crew

In a shared shoot, click the present button at the top to present to everyone who has the shoot open. They follow your view as you move around and switch setups. Anyone can leave by panning or with Leave, and rejoin from the bar at the top. You can see who's following, run a poll, start dot voting (everyone clicks objects or cards to give them dots; select objects or cards first to vote on just those), and start a timer that everyone sees. Everyone can send emoji reactions. Poll and dot-vote results are saved to the board as a note.

New
Play the animatic from the board

Storyboards with two or more frames now have a play button on the card, showing the running time. Click it to watch the animatic with its music and voice-over without opening the editor, and press Esc to go back to the board. It also works for people with view-only access.

New
Invite friends

Account → Invite friends gives you a personal link. When someone signs up with it, you both get one more shoot on the Free plan, for up to 5 friends (up to 8 shoots for free). You can see who joined, and you get a notification when a friend signs up. Everything stays unlimited during the alpha; the extra shoots count from the day the Free plan starts.

New
Connect Claude to Set Plot

Add Set Plot as a connector in Claude and your AI agent can read your setups, build lighting plans, move and adjust lights, add notes and mind maps, and count your gear. It works in the setup you have open, keeps going in the same setup when you ask for more, and asks when it isn't sure which setup you mean. While it works, everyone with the setup open sees its cursor, named after you ("Anna's AI agent"), and can't edit the objects it is changing. Every AI change gets a small ✦ badge so you can accept or undo it, and editing the object yourself counts as rejecting it. Project admins can set a shoot to Suggest only (Share → AI agents): the agent's changes then appear as ghosts until someone applies them.

New
Set Plot Academy

Free lessons on film lighting and shoot planning at plot.malikmotion.com/academy: three-point, Rembrandt, butterfly and split lighting, interview, product and window-light setups, colour temperature, the 180-degree rule, lighting diagrams and shot lists. Every lesson has a lighting diagram you can move around, and Open in Set Plot adds the setup to your own plots. A Learn Set Plot section teaches the app itself, with tips, tricks and every keyboard shortcut: getting started, lights and grip, rooms and locations, notes and mind maps, storyboards, keyframe animation, working with your crew and exporting.

New
Invites by email

When you add someone to a shoot, they now get an email. People with an account get a link to open the shoot and a notification in the app. People without one get a link to sign up with their email filled in, then choose a password, and the shoot is waiting for them.

Fixed
Notifications when you're added to a shoot

The notification that someone added you to a shared shoot wasn't being saved. It now shows up in your notifications.

10 November 2026

New
Call sheet, shot list and gear list as PDF

Export → Call sheet, shot list and gear makes one PDF for the shoot day. The call sheet has call and wrap times, location, hospital, weather, a schedule of your setups, crew with phone numbers and call times, and the cast. The shot list shows every storyboard frame with a thumbnail, shot size, camera, lens, movement, action and dialogue. The gear list counts lights, modifiers, gels, grip and cameras across the shoot and adds power per setup. Add the lighting plots if you like. Details you type are saved with the shoot.

Fixed
Comment cards behave again

The reply box is a proper field again and only shows when you point at a comment. Names no longer squeeze into the corner, everything lines up with the text, and resolving is a single tick at the top.

Changed
The editor saves on its own

The drawing and storyboard editor now saves automatically while you work, so the Clear, Cancel and Done buttons are gone. Press Esc or click Board to go back. Undo still takes back any change, and Clear Canvas is in the Edit menu.

New
Report a bug or suggest a feature

What’s new now has a place to report a bug or suggest a feature, in the app and on the What’s new page. Every message goes straight to us.

Improved
Make animatic when you're ready

A single drawing keeps the full canvas. Once a storyboard has two or more frames, a Make animatic button appears, and the timeline and media pool slide in when you click it.

New
Animate layers with keyframes

Click Animate on a layer and it slides onto the timeline as its own track. Open it to keyframe Position, Scale, Rotation and Opacity with stopwatches, like After Effects. Drag keyframes to change their timing, press U to show the keyframed properties, and open the graph editor to shape the speed with Easy Ease (F9) or by dragging the handles. Drag tracks up or down to put layers in front or behind, and drag two layers on a track into each other to cross-fade between them.

New
A timeline that edits like DaVinci Resolve

The storyboard timeline is rebuilt as an edit page: timecode ruler, frames on V1 and audio on A1–A3, a playhead you can scrub, and playback right in the viewer. Click the timeline and the Resolve shortcuts work: Space and J/K/L to play, I/O for in and out, B to blade, ⌘B to split, Shift+Backspace to ripple delete, M for markers, T to roll an edit, N for snapping and Shift+Z to zoom to fit. Trims and deletes keep the audio in sync.

New
Media Pool with bins

A media pool sits beside the timeline. Import photos and audio, sort them into bins, and drag them onto the timeline (a photo becomes a new frame) or onto the canvas. Everyone on the shoot shares the same media pool.

New
Tag objects in the chat

Type # in the chat to point at an object or card. The tag keeps the object's current name, and clicking it finds the object on the plot, highlights it and selects it.

Improved
Chat moved to the bottom right

The chat now sits in the bottom right corner and grows as messages come in. Expand it, drag the corner to resize it, or search the whole conversation.

New
Named versions, side by side

Project admins can save named versions of a setup. Anyone can open a saved version read only next to the live setup, drag the divider to resize, and copy objects or cards straight across.

New
View-only links

Share a link that lets anyone look at a setup without an account. They can't change anything, and you can turn the link off any time.

New
Version history

Set Plot keeps snapshots of each shared setup as you work. Open the clock at the top to go back to an earlier version or copy it as a new setup.

New
Comment threads

Reply to a comment, mark it resolved when it's done, and reopen it if it isn't.

New
Reactions

React to chat messages and comments with an emoji.

New
Chat with your crew

Shared setups now have a chat. Open it from the speech bubble at the top. Messages are saved with the setup and arrive live.

New
@mentions and notifications

Type @ in the chat or in a comment to mention someone. They get a notification in the new bell at the top right, and clicking it takes them straight to the message or comment.

Fixed
Cursors no longer disappear

Collaborators' cursors stay visible when they hold still or their connection blinks, show up as soon as you open a setup, and point from the edge of the screen when they're outside your view.

Improved
Selections show up instantly

When someone selects something in a shared setup, the others see the outline and lock straight away instead of after a few seconds.

New
Follow a collaborator

Click someone's picture at the top of a shared setup to follow them. Your view moves along with theirs and zooms so you see everything on their screen, even if yours is smaller. Pan or zoom yourself, or click Stop, to take back control.

Improved
Crew changes glide into place

When someone in a shared setup moves, turns or resizes something, it now glides smoothly to its new place instead of jumping, without a notice popping up every time.

New
Sign in with Google

Create an account or sign in with your Google account in one click.

New
Delete your account

Account → Delete account removes your account and your work. Shared projects you started are handed to another member, so your crew keeps them.

Changed
An account is now needed

Set Plot now asks you to sign in. Your setups are always saved to your account and follow you to every device.

New
See others move things live

In shared setups you now see objects move, turn and change as your crew edits them, and their cursors glide smoothly.

Fixed
Setups stay with their account

Signing in to a different account in the same browser no longer shows the previous account's setups.

New
Clearer sign-in and sign-up

Separate screens for signing in and creating an account, password reset, and a few quick questions about your work when you join.

New
Edit locks

Whatever a collaborator has selected is outlined with their name, and nobody else can change it until they let go.

New
Live cursors

See where everyone in a shared setup is pointing, with their name and colour, and who is online.

What’s new

New features, improvements and fixes in Set Plot, newest first.

Open Set Plot

10 November 2026

Improved
Selections show up instantly

When someone selects something in a shared setup, the others see the outline and lock straight away instead of after a few seconds.

New
Follow a collaborator

Click someone's picture at the top of a shared setup to follow them: your view moves and zooms along with theirs. Pan or zoom yourself, or click Stop, to take back control.

Improved
Crew changes glide into place

When someone in a shared setup moves, turns or resizes something, it now glides smoothly to its new place instead of jumping, without a notice popping up every time.

New
Sign in with Google

Create an account or sign in with your Google account in one click.

New
Delete your account

Account → Delete account removes your account and your work. Shared projects you started are handed to another member, so your crew keeps them.

Changed
An account is now needed

Set Plot now asks you to sign in. Your setups are always saved to your account and follow you to every device.

New
See others move things live

In shared setups you now see objects move, turn and change as your crew edits them, and their cursors glide smoothly.

Fixed
Setups stay with their account

Signing in to a different account in the same browser no longer shows the previous account's setups.

New
Clearer sign-in and sign-up

Separate screens for signing in and creating an account, password reset, and a few quick questions about your work when you join.

New
Edit locks

Whatever a collaborator has selected is outlined with their name, and nobody else can change it until they let go.

New
Live cursors

See where everyone in a shared setup is pointing, with their name and colour, and who is online.

New
Shared projects

Invite your crew to a shoot. Choose who can view, edit or invite, and make others admin. The person who started the project always stays admin.

New
AI voice-over

Generate a voice-over for each frame from text, hear voice samples first, and use tags like [laughs] or [whispers] for delivery.

New

Record voice-over with your microphone and add music or sound-effect tracks that play with the animatic.

New
Animatic

Play your storyboard as an animatic, set timing by tapping along, drag clip lengths on the timeline, add transitions, and present full screen.

New
Storyboard rows

Organise frames in rows with titles, and drag frames between rows.

New
Brand kit and palettes

Keep a brand's colours with the project and pull a palette straight from a photo.

New
Cloud sync

Your setups, photos and audio sync to your account, stored in the EU.
